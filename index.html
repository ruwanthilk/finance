Profit & loss by platform

Daily profit & loss (selected month)

Daily P/L = trade profit or loss minus fees, in USD. Deposits and withdrawals are not counted as profit.

Trading log (selected month)

Trade = profit (+) or loss (−). Deposit/Withdrawal = money moved to/from the broker. Trading equity = net deposits + net P/L, so don't also add it as an account.

Other investments

Accounts & cards

Cash advance (loan from card, cash received)

Use this when you take cash from a credit card. The card balance goes up (you owe it back) and the cash is received into the account you pick. It is not income or spending. Any fee is recorded as an expense.

Move money between accounts

Not income or spending. Takes the amount from one account and adds it to the other. Withdrawal: bank → cash. Deposit: cash → bank.
